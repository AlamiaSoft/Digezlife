## Brand & Style

This design system serves a mobile-first progressive web application tailored for Pakistani multi-generational households managing monthly budgets, daily groceries (*sauda-salf*), domestic help salaries, and utility bills. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Intent
- **Warm & Welcoming (*Apna-pan*):** Eliminates cold, sterile corporate fintech aesthetics. It embraces warmth through rich cream tones, organic geometry, and conversational Pakistani domestic contexts.
- **Steadfast & Trustworthy (*Aitabaar*):** Deep forest green anchors household wealth with stability, security, and integrity, evoking generational wisdom and prosperity.
- **Effortless Clarity (*Sahoolat*):** Simplifies multi-tier family spending into instant, glanceable summaries that can be understood by young professionals and elderly parents alike.

### Design Movement: Modern Warm Tactility
The style merges **Modern Scandinavian warmth** with **contemporary subcontinental fintech**. It avoids glossy glassmorphism or abrasive brutalism in favor of soft, pillowed card surfaces, tactile button presses, high-legibility typography, and fine-grained ambient warm shadows. High surface contrast ensures total clarity under bright outdoor sunlight across varied mobile displays.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic Pakistani domestic life: deep evergreen foliage (*Sabz*), sun-baked terracotta, golden mustard seed (*Sarson*), and warm unbleached parchment (*Kora Kaghaz*).

### Functional Color Roles
- **Primary (`#064e3b`):** Deep Forest Green. Anchors top app bars, primary actions, prominent financial balances, and verified statuses.
- **Secondary (`#b45309`):** Golden Ochre / Warm Amber. Used for grocery category accents (*Sauda*), bill pending flags, budget caps, and attention items.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`):** Rich Emerald Green. Specifically handles paid receipts, surplus wallet balances, and successful cash transfers.
- **Neutral (`#1c1917`):** Deep Charcoal. High-contrast primary text on light cream surfaces, replacing stark pitch-black for reduced eye strain.
- **Alert / Overdue (`#dc2626`):** Deep Crimson / Terracotta Red. Dedicated strictly to utility disconnection warnings, overspent envelopes, and critical alerts.
- **Surface / Background:** Tinted cream (`#f7f5ef`) for application canvases, with pure white (`#ffffff`) for elevated interactive cards.
- **Subtle Stroke & Dividers:** Warm muted stone (`#e7e5e4`) to structure cards without harsh contrast.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes exceptional legibility across bilingual-aware latin strings, financial figures, and product terms.

- **Primary Typeface (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Chosen for its humanist geometry, open counters, and warm, rounded friendliness that prevents cold transactional stiffness.
- **Secondary Typeface (Inter):** Applied across all labels, numerical amounts, and data-dense tables to guarantee tabular numeral alignment and instant scanning of Pakistani Rupee figures (e.g., `PKR 148,250`).
- **Currency Presentation:** The currency symbol `PKR` must always precede numbers separated by a non-breaking space with tabular numerals enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). Large metrics utilize `currency-display` with a slightly lighter font weight on the currency code than the figure itself.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, targeting single-handed thumb operation on screens ranging from compact smartphones to modern edge-to-edge displays, scaling up cleanly to tablets and desktop monitors.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column fluid view with strict `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Core interactions sit within bottom safe zones to avoid thumb-stretch.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Splits home summaries into a dual-column pattern (e.g., Bills list on left, Grocery ledger on right).
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column grid centered within a maximum container width of `1200px`. Generous `3rem` margins to prevent visual diffusion.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Multiples of 4px and 8px govern component heights and vertical flow. Form inputs, bottom sheets, and action items retain a minimum touch target height of 48px to accommodate quick physical interaction during market shopping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered warm surfaces supported by diffused ambient drop shadows rather than industrial gray drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Warm Cream background (`#f7f5ef`). Ground plane for all scrollable views.
- **Surface Cards (Level 1):** Solid white surface (`#ffffff`) surrounded by a subtle border (`1px solid #e7e5e4`) and an extra-soft tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive / Focus (Level 2):** Hovered cards or elevated list items: `box-shadow: 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Action Elements (Level 3):** Bottom navigation bars, the central floating action button, sticky category filters, and bottom action sheets: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.15), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`. Shadows on primary floating elements pick up an ultra-subtle tint of the deep forest primary hue.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses physical approachability and comfort. Generous, friendly radii make finance feel approachable rather than intimidating.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** Standard base radius of `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons and inputs; pill-shaped (`9999px`) for quick-filter chips, utility badges, and status pills.
- **Cards & Summary Tiles:** `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / 16px) creates a soft, tactile boundary for individual bills (e.g., K-Electric, Sui Northern Gas) and grocery lists.
- **Modals, Drawers & Bottom Sheets:** `rounded-t-3xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for pull-up quick entry forms (e.g., recording a daily *sauda* expense or maid's advance).
- **Floating Action Button (FAB):** Circular (`rounded-full`), anchoring the center of the mobile navigation bar.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep forest green background (`#064e3b`), crisp white text, `0.75rem` vertical padding, bold weight. Active state reveals a soft downward depression (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** Warm cream tone with an amber or forest border (`1.5px solid #064e3b`), neutral text.
- **Floating Center Action Button:** Raised circular emerald/forest button (`56px` diameter) elevated above the bottom navigation dock, featuring an intuitive plus icon for instant transaction entry.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with light tonal backgrounds. 
  - *Paid / Complete:* Light emerald tint (`#ecfdf5`), dark emerald text (`#065f46`).
  - *Pending / Due:* Warm amber tint (`#fffbeb`), dark ochre text (`#92400e`).
  - *Urgent / Overdue:* Soft rose tint (`#fef2f2`), deep crimson text (`#991b1b`).
- **Category Filter Chips:** Horizontal scrolling pills for household buckets: *Sabzi Mandi*, *Chakki/Atta*, *Doodh/Bakery*, *Utilities*, *Ghar Kharacha*.

### Cards
- **Utility Bill Card:** Displays bill provider monogram/logo (e.g., K-Electric, SSGC, PTCL, SNGPL), due date badge, formatted payable amount (e.g., `PKR 8,420`), and a one-tap mark-as-paid CTA.
- **Pantry / Sauda Card:** Micro-ledger card with clear checkmark toggle, item title (e.g., *Daal Chana 2kg*, *Chakki Atta 10kg*, *Rooh Afza 800ml*), price field, and dynamic strikethrough upon completion.

### Form Inputs
- **Amount Field:** High-impact numeric input with a static prefix (`PKR`), displaying oversized numbers (`28px`), auto-formatting thousands with commas.
- **Text & Dropdown Inputs:** Subtle warm stone border (`#d6d3d1`), light cream background fill (`#fdfcf7`), turning forest green on active focus with an ambient ring.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Household Feed Item:** Left-aligned icon or avatar showing family contributor (e.g., *Abba*, *Ammi*, *Bilal*), title, timestamp, and right-aligned debit/credit amount in bold tabular figures. Separated by hairline dividers (`#f5f5f4`).

### Navigation
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Anchored to bottom with safe-area padding. Houses Home, Sauda List, Split Center FAB, Bills, and Household Settings with active icons highlighted in forest green and muted slate for inactive states.
